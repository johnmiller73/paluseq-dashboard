How to read this dashboard

Prevalence is the share of samples genotyped at a codon that carry the mutant allele (mutant samples ÷ samples genotyped), per province. Denominators differ by codon because not every sample was genotyped at every position.

k13 marker groups follow the WHO classification of markers of artemisinin partial resistance: validated, candidate and potential. 'Other' k13 changes are listed in the detail table only.

Wild-type rows such as mdr1-Asn86, mdr1-Asp1246 and dhps-Gly437 are reported as in the PALUSEQ summaries, where the wild-type allele is the one associated with reduced drug susceptibility (e.g. mdr1 N86 with lumefantrine).

About the data: all values are synthetic. Sample counts and mutant counts were drawn at random around marker frequencies of a realistic order of magnitude, for the nine provinces shown. Province names are real; the numbers, the report date and any patterns between provinces are not. Replace this file with real PALUSEQ data before using the dashboard for analysis.
